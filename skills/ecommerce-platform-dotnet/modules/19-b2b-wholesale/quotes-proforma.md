# 19 · Quotes & proforma

## Model
- `Quote`: `Id`, `TenantId`, `OrganizationId`/customer, lines (variant, qty, negotiated
  price), validity date, status (Draft | Sent | Accepted | Rejected | Expired), notes.
- Generated by staff or requested by the buyer ("request a quote").

## Workflow
1. Buyer requests a quote (cart → quote) or staff drafts one.
2. Staff adjust prices/terms → send.
3. Buyer accepts (within validity) → `QuoteAccepted` → convert to an order (04) at the
   quoted prices; or rejects/expires.
- Proforma invoice (04) issued from the quote/order for payment or credit processing.

## Rules
- Accepted quote locks prices into the order snapshot.
- Validity enforced; expired quotes can't convert.
- Permissioned; audited; org-scoped.

## API surface
- Request/create quote; edit/send; accept/reject; convert to order; generate proforma.
